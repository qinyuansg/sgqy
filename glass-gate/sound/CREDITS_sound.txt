  is at exactly 120 BPM, the tempo of the video).


SOUND EFFECTS

  No recordings and no sound libraries were used. Every effect in the film (metal thunks, clacks, clicks,
  snaps, glass clinks and slides, gear ticks, spring, screwdriver, servo, whooshes, riser, drop impact,
  power-up, shimmer, UI chime, card tap, reader beep, final hit) and the room they share were synthesised
  from scratch for this project in Python with numpy and scipy: modal synthesis for glass and metal,
  filtered noise for contact, air and friction. Code: sound/sfxlib.py.

  The original plan was to use sounds from pixabay.com. That site answered the production machine with a
  bot challenge, which was not circumvented; so nothing from Pixabay is in the film and no Pixabay credit
  is due.

  Sound design and mix: generated by sound/mix.py from shared/timeline.json.
