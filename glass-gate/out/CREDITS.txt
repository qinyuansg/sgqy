CLEAR PASSAGE — credits and licences
====================================

Everything on screen was modelled, shaded, lit, animated and rendered from scratch for this film in
Blender 5.3 (Cycles) by scripts in this repository. No downloaded 3D models were used. The product,
the name "HYALO G1" and every label, badge and screen text are made up; no real brand appears.

SOUND EFFECTS  (in the film)

  No recordings and no sound libraries. Every effect (metal thunks, clacks, clicks, snaps, glass clinks
  and slides, gear ticks, spring, screwdriver, servo, whooshes, riser, drop impact, power-up, shimmer,
  UI chime, card tap, reader beep, final hit) and the room they share were synthesised for this film in
  Python with numpy and scipy (sound/sfxlib.py). No credit is due to anyone.

  The brief asked for Pixabay sound effects. pixabay.com answered this machine with a bot challenge,
  which was not circumvented, so nothing from Pixabay is in the film.
  Real recordings can be dropped into sound/sfx_override/<name>.wav and sound/mix.py will use them.
