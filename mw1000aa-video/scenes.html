Considerations to manage

False alarms

  • • Clothing folds, sweat, belts, buttons
  • • More pat-downs and officer workload
  • → divest guidance, ATR tuning, training

Inclusion & accommodation

  • • Prosthetics and medical devices
  • • Religious and cultural garments
  • → respectful, well-trained alternative procedures

Privacy, data & cyber

  • • Which images (if any) are stored?
  • • For how long, and who can access them?
  • • Network security and patching

Supplier & geopolitical risk

  • • Founded 1997, Tsinghua University origins
  • • US Entity List since 2020
  • • EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation scrutiny since 2024
