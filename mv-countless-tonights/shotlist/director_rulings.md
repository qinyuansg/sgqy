# 导演裁定 · Director's rulings (v1, after prompt-pass QA)

These rulings close the open questions raised by the prompt writers and QA (`delivery/prompt_pack_zh_en.md` §7.2).
Where a ruling touches a shot, `shots.json` carries it in that shot's `director_ruling` field. **Rulings beat the bible
where they conflict; the shot list beats the bible on on-screen layout.**

## Global
1. **Mirror rule — approved as merged.** Era images seen in glass are composited/staged *as seen on screen* (the layout
   written in the shot list wins). Reflections of the present (the restorer's own reflection, the future viewer) behave as
   true mirrors. Bible §8.3-4 is superseded for era images.
2. **Cinematic cheats approved:** moon position/height (S018, S023–S027), moonlight reach in the gallery, the diamond-pane
   foreground plate (S057), chapel light from behind the mother (S058), rack focus past the vitrine edge rather than
   through it (S061), light from the arched doorways in S078, through-view + reflection layering in S081. The camera tells the
   truth of feeling; geography stays plausible at a glance.
3. **Window-bay coverage = BAY_3Q** (three-quarter from the bay's south-west corner) for S062–S077: guard screen-right by the
   east window, restorer screen-left; the back view (S066 end, S079) is the reverse angle.
4. **Text props:** compass bearings, letters, ledgers, passes stay illegible / soft in previz and AI generation; a calligraphy
   plate is inserted in post for any macro that must read.
5. **Supplementary refs `XREF_*`** (elder's hand, clerk's hand, child's feet, thermos, steamer deck) are registered as part of the
   reference library (prompt pack §5); thermos = dull unbranded steel flask with a scuffed navy cup-lid.
6. **Bible sync:** S017 uses the lab's arched window (not G3b); the future coat is **folded** (shot list wins); T24 is dropped on
   purpose (S071's raised hand is the rhyme); candle states: B = 11 cm (S016–S017 start) → C = 6 cm after the S017 dissolve,
   C in CH1/CH2, a fresh stub is never shown.
