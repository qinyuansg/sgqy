# The Butterfly Garden — AI Video Prompt Pack

A shot-by-shot prompt kit for turning your daughter's drawing into a cinematic AI video (Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, Hailuo or similar). It follows the same storyboard as the finished film in this folder, so you can regenerate any shot, make a longer version, or produce new episodes with the same characters.

---

## 0. How to get her characters to stay *hers*

1. **Use image-to-video, not text-only.** Upload a reference image for every shot. The cut-out sprites in `assets/` (`dino.png`, `butterfly.png`, `tree.png`, `iceman.png`, `police.png`, `icewall.png`) are clean character sheets made from her actual drawing. For a scene with several characters, use `page.jpg` (the whole cleaned-up drawing) as the reference.
2. **Paste the Style Bible (§1) at the end of every prompt.** It is the glue that keeps every clip in the same world.
3. **Keep clips short (5–8 s) and generate 2–4 takes per shot.** Pick the best take and stitch them in CapCut, iMovie or DaVinci.
4. **Add the music last** (§5) so the cuts land on the beat. The theme runs at 120 BPM, so every 2 seconds is one bar: cut on bar lines.
5. If a tool drifts toward realism (scaly skin, real fire), add more words from the negative prompt (§4) and the phrase *"flat paper cut-out, child's marker drawing"*.

---

## 1. Style Bible (append to every prompt)

> Style: a 3½-year-old's marker-and-crayon drawing brought to life as a cinematic 2.5D paper-cut-out storybook. Every character is a flat paper sticker with a white scissor-cut border and a soft drop shadow, wobbly hand-drawn marker outlines, visible crayon texture and cream paper grain. Layered parallax scenery like a pop-up book, soft volumetric light, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain. Saturated candy-pastel palette with strong color contrast. Whimsical, cozy, safe for toddlers, never truly scary. Smooth, bouncy "squash-and-stretch" motion. 16:9, 24 fps.

## 3. Shot List — 15 shots, about 2 minutes

Each block: **prompt** (copy-paste), then camera, sound and on-screen text. Add the Style Bible to every prompt.

### Shot 1 — "The Drawing Wakes Up" · 6 s · ref: `photo.jpg`
**Prompt:** Top-down shot of a child's two-page marker drawing lying on a grey foam play mat: a dinosaur breathing red flames, a blue ice tower, a pink butterfly, a flowering tree, a blue ice man and a little police car. A glowing golden star swoops across the page leaving glitter; wherever it passes the paper turns bright and clean. One by one each drawing peels up off the paper like a sticker, casting a soft shadow, and its white areas fill with soft color: mint tree, pink butterfly, pale-green dinosaur.
**Camera:** slow push-in, slight drift. **Sound:** music-box sparkle glissando; a magical chime as each character lifts. **Text:** *The Butterfly Garden* in bubbly pink-to-orange letters.

### Shot 2 — "Into the Garden" · 4 s · ref: `butterfly.png`
**Prompt:** The camera dives toward the pink butterfly on the page, faster and faster, until its patterned wing fills the frame, then a soft white sparkle flash transitions into a sunny paper-cut garden.
**Camera:** accelerating dolly-in, whip into flash. **Sound:** rising whoosh and sparkle burst.

### Shot 3 — "The Butterfly Garden" · 8 s · refs: `tree.png`, `butterfly.png`
**Prompt:** Wide establishing shot of a cheerful paper-cut garden: a smiling crayon sun with blushing cheeks and spinning rays, puffy paper clouds, rolling crayon-textured green hills, mint-green flowering trees. Little pink, red and orange flowers pop up out of the grass one after another like popcorn, each with a bounce. The pink butterfly flutters in from the right, leaving a trail of sparkles and tiny red hearts.
**Camera:** slow crane down and push-in, parallax between hills, trees and flowers. **Sound:** the Butterfly Theme on music box, pizzicato bass, soft shaker; a "pop" for each flower, tuned to the melody.

### Shot 4 — "Loop-de-loop" · 6 s · ref: `butterfly.png`
**Prompt:** Close-up tracking shot of the butterfly doing a joyful loop-de-loop between flowering trees; hearts float off her wings and drift upward; dappled sunlight. She glides down and lands on a giant pink flower, wings slowly opening and closing.
**Camera:** follow cam, ending in a macro close-up. **Sound:** theme continues; a sparkle chord on landing.

### Shot 5 — "Uh-oh!" · 5 s · refs: `tree.png`, `butterfly.png`
**Prompt:** The music stops with a record scratch. The ground trembles, the flowers shiver, the butterfly jumps up startled with a yellow "!" above her head. Purple storm clouds roll across the sky and cover the sun. BOOM, BOOM: footsteps shake leaves and petals off the trees.
**Camera:** sudden pull-back, rhythmic camera shake on every footstep. **Sound:** vinyl stop, "uh-oh" two-note toot, deep footstep thuds, heartbeat. **Text:** *Uh-oh!*, *BOOM!*

### Shot 6 — "Shadow in the Storm" · 6 s · ref: `dino.png`
**Prompt:** Whip pan left across the hills to reveal a giant dark silhouette of the dinosaur stomping in, one round eye glowing red. A crayon lightning bolt cracks and, in the flash, the dinosaur is revealed in color: black marker outlines, green-and-red spikes, white zigzag teeth, purple eyelashes, a blue teardrop, holding a red apple.
**Camera:** whip pan, then a slow low-angle push-in. **Sound:** thunder crack and rumble, dramatic timpani hit.

### Shot 7 — "ROAR!" · 4 s · ref: `dino.png`
**Prompt:** Low-angle close-up as the dinosaur throws its head back and ROARS, its big mouth opening wide to show white zigzag teeth; the wind ripples the grass; the screen shakes. Dramatic, but funny rather than scary.
**Camera:** fast push-in, then hold with camera shake. **Sound:** big cartoon roar plus orchestra hit. **Text:** a giant shaking *ROAR!*

### Shot 8 — "Fire!" · 6 s · refs: `dino.png`, `tree.png`
**Prompt:** The dinosaur takes a huge breath (its belly puffs up), then blows a long stream of cartoon fire: red marker-outlined flame tongues glowing yellow and orange. The fire rushes toward the garden, lighting everything orange. The butterfly zips away to hide behind a tree, trembling; nearby flowers droop sadly. Nothing actually burns.
**Camera:** pans with the fire stream from the mouth to the garden. **Sound:** inhale whoosh, roaring fire crackle, dark low-strings ostinato. **Text:** *WHOOSH!*, *Oh no!*

### Shot 9 — "Ice Man Arrives" · 6 s · ref: `iceman.png`
**Prompt:** Snowflakes begin to fall. A magical chime. Ice Man, a tall blue crayon figure with a green smile holding a crescent-moon ice blade, skates in from the right on a glittering ice trail, spins and stops. Light rays burst behind him in a hero pose as he raises the crescent, glowing icy blue.
**Camera:** tracking shot, then a dramatic zoom into a hero close-up. **Sound:** shimmering bells, ice-skate swishes, a brass fanfare of the Butterfly Theme. **Text:** *ICE MAN!*

### Shot 10 — "The Ice Wall" · 7 s · refs: `iceman.png`, `icewall.png`, `dino.png`
**Prompt:** Ice Man jumps and slams his crescent into the ground; a ring of ice crystals races across the grass and a tall tower of blue crayon ice blocks shoots up out of the ground block by block, sparkling. At that exact moment the dinosaur blasts fire, and the flames splash against the ice wall and spray up and down in bright orange bursts, turning into big puffy white steam clouds. The fire cannot get through.
**Camera:** wide side view, a slight dolly. **Sound:** ice-block chimes climbing a scale, impact boom, sizzling steam, a heroic march. **Text:** *SIZZLE!*

### Shot 11 — "Wee-woo!" · 7 s · ref: `police.png`
**Prompt:** Red and blue light sweeps across the scene. The little white police car with a blue outline, a red siren and three tiny officers zooms in with speed lines and dust, bounces and skids to a stop. A small icy net cannon pops out of its roof with a "boing" and swivels to aim at the dinosaur. A big countdown pops up: 3… 2… 1…
**Camera:** tracking with the car, then a punchy close-up on the cannon. **Sound:** "wee-woo" siren, engine vroom, tyre skid, funky chase groove, wood-block ticks for the countdown. **Text:** *Wee-woo! Wee-woo!*, *3 · 2 · 1 · GO!*

### Shot 12 — "The Ice Net" · 7 s · refs: `police.png`, `dino.png`
**Prompt:** In slow motion the cannon fires a glowing ice-net ball high over the ice wall, trailing sparkles; it unfolds into a huge shimmering dome-shaped net of icy blue lines and drops over the dinosaur, pinned down by little ice stakes. Frost crystals spread, the fire fizzles out, and the dinosaur coughs out one small grey smoke ring.
**Camera:** follows the net through the air in slow motion, then a punch-in on impact. **Sound:** launch thump, rising whistle, reverse-cymbal swell, a big "GOTCHA" orchestra hit, ice crackle. **Text:** *GOTCHA!*

### Shot 13 — "Sorry!" · 9 s · refs: `dino.png`, `butterfly.png`
**Prompt:** Inside the sparkly ice net the dinosaur's lip wobbles; then big cartoon fountain tears squirt out of its eye, and a puddle forms. It raises a little white flag and a speech bubble pops up: "Sorry!". The butterfly flies over, gently touches the dinosaur's nose, and a burst of hearts appears. The tears stop and the dinosaur blushes pink and smiles shyly.
**Camera:** close-up on the face, then a gentle pull-back. **Sound:** sad trombone "wah-wah-wah-waaah", cartoon "waaah" sobs, tear plips, sniffles, then a harp glissando and soft music box. **Text:** *Waaah!*, *Sorry!*

### Shot 14 — "HOORAY!" · 12 s · ref: `page.jpg`
**Prompt:** The storm clouds roll away, the smiling sun returns and a crayon rainbow arcs across the sky. Flowers bloom across the whole meadow in a wave; confetti made of tiny hearts and flowers falls; fireworks of sparkles burst. Everyone dances to the beat: Ice Man hops, the police car bounces with party-colored lights, the trees sway, the dinosaur sways happily in its net with hearts above its head, and the butterfly loops around everyone.
**Camera:** wide, slowly drifting, with a little zoom punch on every 4th beat. **Sound:** full party version of the Butterfly Theme (claps, drums, brass, glockenspiel, party horns, firework pops). **Text:** rainbow *HOORAY!*

### Shot 15 — "Back to the Page" · 8 s · ref: `page.jpg`
**Prompt:** The butterfly flies straight toward the camera until her wings fill the screen; a soft white flash; we are back on the real drawing, and each character flies back into its place on the paper with a little sparkle. Hearts float up where the dinosaur's fire used to be. "The End" appears with a heart.
**Camera:** slow pull-back over the drawing. **Sound:** music box plays the first line of the theme; a final shimmer. **Text:** *The End*, plus her name, e.g. *Drawn by Mia ♥*

## 5. Music & sound

**Music prompt (Suno / Udio / similar, instrumental):**
> Instrumental children's cinematic score, 2 minutes, 120 BPM, no vocals. Main motif: a cheerful, singable 8-bar "Butterfly Theme" in C major on music box and glockenspiel with pizzicato bass and soft shaker. Then a record-scratch stop into tense low strings, heartbeat and timpani with cartoon thunder; a dark low-string ostinato in C minor; a bright brass-fanfare version of the Butterfly Theme in D major with snare march; a funky police-chase groove with siren; a slow-motion shimmering swell into a big orchestra hit; a comic sad-trombone moment and gentle harp; and a joyful party version of the theme in F major with claps, drums, brass and party horns. It ends quietly on music box. Playful, magical, Pixar-short energy, toddler-friendly.

**Sound effects to layer:** sparkle chimes, flower pops (tuned to the melody), footsteps BOOM, thunder, cartoon roar, fire whoosh and crackle, ice-block chimes, steam sizzle, wee-woo siren, engine vroom and skid, boing, wood-block countdown ticks, launch thump and whistle, GOTCHA hit, ice crackle, smoke-ring cough, sad trombone, "waaah", tear plips, sniffles, a "mwah" kiss, harp glissando, birds, fireworks, party horns.

## 7. Why it works for a 3½-year-old (and what to keep in any remake)

- **Her own drawing is the star.** The video opens and closes on the real page, so she sees that *she* made it.
- **One catchy melody, repeated.** The Butterfly Theme comes back as music box, brass march and party song, which is predictable and satisfying.
- **Sound words she can shout:** BOOM! ROAR! WHOOSH! Wee-woo! GOTCHA! HOORAY!
- **Call-and-response moments:** roar along, count 3-2-1, say sorry, dance.
- **Danger with a soft landing.** The dinosaur is dramatic but funny, nothing is really hurt, and he says sorry and gets a kiss.
- **Big color contrast:** a candy-pastel garden, then a purple storm with glowing fire and ice, then a rainbow finale.
- **Short.** About 2 minutes is the sweet spot for this age; make *new episodes* rather than longer ones.
